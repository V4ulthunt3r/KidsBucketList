The idea

Not: “What should we do today?”
But: “What do we want to have done together?”

Kids Bucket List isn't an app for quick entertainment. It stays with you for years – from toddler days to adulthood. Some things fit today. Some you deliberately keep for later.

🌱 Today

For the next moment

A pajama party, an afternoon of crafts, a book to read aloud. Small things you can do right now.

🚀 Soon

When the time is right

Playing your first proper game together, a movie night with your favourite film, the first concert.

🏔️ Someday

Saved for the right moment

Watching The Matrix, crossing the Alps, the big trip. It doesn't need to be age-appropriate yet – just captured.

What the app does

Collect, share, remember

A wishlist, a memory keeper and your shared interests – all in one place.

⭐

A bucket list for life

💭

Interest worlds, not ages

🔎

Real data & templates

Search books, find films, discover craft ideas – and add them to your list with a single tap.

📊

Statistics & highlights

Progress per child, favourite categories and your loveliest moments at a glance.
